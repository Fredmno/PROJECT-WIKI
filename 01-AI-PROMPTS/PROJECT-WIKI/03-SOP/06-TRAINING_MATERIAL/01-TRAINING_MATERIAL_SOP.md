# Purpose

This SOP defines the standard process for creating, reviewing, maintaining, delivering, and retiring training materials within the PROJECT-WIKI repository.

The objective is to:

- improve knowledge transfer
- accelerate onboarding
- standardize training content
- reduce dependency on individual knowledge holders
- support continuous learning
- preserve organizational knowledge

---

# Scope

This SOP applies to:

- onboarding materials
- process training
- application training
- project training
- SOP walkthroughs
- change training
- user guides
- quick reference guides
- workshop materials
- recorded training sessions

---

## Technical Owner

Responsible for:

- validating technical content

- knowledge organization

## Learner

Responsible for:

- completing assigned training
- providing feedback
- identifying knowledge gaps

## Objective

# Step 3 – Review Training Content

## Objective

Validate training quality.

## Review Areas
