\# Purpose



This folder contains ServiceNow-related documentation, governance standards, process guides, workflows, references, and operational knowledge.



The objective is to provide a centralized source of truth for:



\- Demand Management

\- Change Management

\- Incident Management

\- Problem Management

\- Service Catalog

\- Request Fulfillment

\- IT Governance

\- ITSM Processes

\- Service Management Operations
